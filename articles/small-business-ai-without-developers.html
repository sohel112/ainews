How Small Businesses Can Use AI Today (No Developers Required)

By AI Brief · 2026-10-08 · 8 min read

A practical playbook: customer support, marketing, and admin — the three places AI pays for itself fastest in a small business.

You don't need an AI strategy — you need three quick wins. 1. Customer support: an AI chatbot trained on your FAQs handles the repetitive 70% of questions overnight and on weekends. Tools for this now require zero coding. 2. Marketing: AI drafts product descriptions, social posts, and email campaigns in your brand voice; you edit and approve.

3. Admin: AI transcribes meetings, summarizes long threads, drafts invoices follow-ups, and organizes messy spreadsheets. Start with one workflow, measure the hours saved, then expand.

Why it matters: Small businesses can't out-hire competitors, but they can out-automate them. Our take: the winning approach is boring on purpose — pick the single most time-consuming repetitive task, automate it with an off-the-shelf AI tool, and bank the hours. Repeat monthly.
