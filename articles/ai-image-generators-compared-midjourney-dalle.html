AI Image Generators Compared: Midjourney vs DALL-E vs Stable Diffusion vs Flux

By AI Brief · 2026-10-09 · 7 min read

Which AI image tool should you use? We compare the big four on quality, ease of use, and price.

Midjourney remains the king of beautiful, artistic images — its aesthetic sense is unmatched, though it lives inside Discord (or its newer web app) and has no free tier. DALL-E (via ChatGPT) is the most convenient: describe what you want in plain language while chatting, and it iterates with you. Best for beginners.

Stable Diffusion is the tinkerer's choice: free, open, and runnable on your own computer, with thousands of community models for every style imaginable — but it needs technical setup. Flux, the newer open contender, has impressed with photorealism and text rendering (AI image tools famously garble words; Flux does better).

Why it matters: Picking wrong wastes subscription money. Our take: casual users should start with DALL-E inside ChatGPT; artists should try Midjourney; technical users who want free and private should go Stable Diffusion or Flux. Text-heavy images? Flux wins.
